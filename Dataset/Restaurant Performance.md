##  Restaurant Performance — STAR Analysis

**Situation:**
The restaurant network generated **₹6.16 Cr in sales**, serving around **100K customers**, with an **average sales/customer of ₹648.70** and **21% repeat customers**. Mall stores contribute the largest share of sales at **41%**, followed by High Street stores at **28%**. 

**Task:**
Identify the strongest restaurant formats, cities, and operating periods to improve **store productivity, customer retention, and sales**.

**Action:**

* Prioritize **Mall and High Street locations**, which contribute the largest sales share.
* Optimize staffing and inventory around the **12–2 PM and 6–8 PM peak hours**.
* Focus weekend campaigns, particularly **Saturday**, when sales are strongest.
* Improve customer retention by targeting the relatively low **21% repeat-customer rate** with loyalty and personalized offers.
* Benchmark lower-performing cities/stores against stronger performers such as **Bengaluru and Hyderabad**. 

**Result:**
The analysis indicates that **store format, peak-hour operations, and customer retention** are the key opportunities. Improving repeat purchases while maximizing capacity during peak periods can help increase **sales per customer and overall restaurant productivity**.
