**Situation:**
California Burrito generated **₹6.16 Cr in sales from 95K orders**, with an **AOV of ₹648.66** and **₹3.55 Cr margin**. Sales remained relatively stable across the year, but some months and states performed significantly better than others. 

**Task:**
Identify the key sales drivers, underperforming periods, and opportunities to improve **revenue, order volume, and profitability**.

**Action:**

* Focus promotional campaigns on **low-performing months such as February**.
* Replicate successful strategies from **March, August, and October** during weaker periods.
* Prioritize growth initiatives in **Maharashtra**, the highest-sales state.
* Optimize discounts (**₹40.20L**) to ensure promotions generate incremental orders without unnecessarily reducing margin.
* Promote high-performing categories, particularly **Beverages and Sides**, to increase basket value. 

**Result:**
The analysis indicates opportunities to **increase order volume during weaker months, improve AOV through cross-selling, and protect margins through better-targeted discounts**. The strongest-performing states and periods can be used as benchmarks for expansion and campaign planning.
